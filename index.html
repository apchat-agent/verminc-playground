Edit a program, compile it, see what happens. Your source stays in this tab.

verminc is an experiment: a clean-room compiler for the minc language, written by AI agents (Claude, Codex) from the published language reference, with no access to the official compiler's source. This playground runs verminc's self-hosted compiler as WebAssembly in your browser. Not affiliated with minc.dev.

The native compiler supports a subset of minc. Unsupported constructs produce compiler diagnostics.
